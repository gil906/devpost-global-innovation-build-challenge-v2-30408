TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

Optimization you can inspect.

CommonTable combines a constraint graph with an exact integer allocation algorithm. It does not use a language model or estimate real-world impact from synthetic data.

01

Make eligible connections

Check category, cold storage, distance, receiving capacity, expiry and opening windows. Travel minutes are rounded up; waiting for a hub to open is allowed.

02

Find the maximum coverage

A flow network connects source, batches, hubs and sink. Batch quantities bound supply; the smaller of hub demand and capacity bounds receipt.

03

Use shorter connections

Successive shortest augmenting paths with residual edges minimize distance per serving at maximum flow. Distances are rounded to the nearest meter for cost.

Why not just choose the closest hub?

In the included fixture, the bakery can serve two hubs but the breakfast club only accepts bakery food. Greedy matching uses up the flexible pantry first. Residual edges let the optimizer reconsider that choice, keeping scarce compatibility available.

What the numbers mean

Servings
An abstract, interchangeable unit in this simulation. No nutrition, weight or meal-equivalence claim is made.
Serving-km
Allocated servings multiplied by direct-leg kilometers, summed over transfers. This is the minimized cost; it is not vehicle distance or emissions.
Loads / trip-km
Ceiling of servings divided by vehicle capacity for each transfer, and one-way distance summed over those loads. These are reported, not optimized. There are no return legs or multi-stop routes.
Coverage
Allocated servings divided by total declared demand. Aggregate coverage can favor some hubs; no fairness policy or minimum per-hub guarantee is imposed.

Boundaries that matter

Same-day windows only. One food category per batch; hub demand is a pooled number across accepted categories. Up to 20 batches and 20 hubs, with coordinates from 0 to 20 km. The planner assumes sufficient parallel vehicles, temperature-controlled transport where needed, and truthful declarations. It does not verify allergens, handling practices, road access or food safety.
